## Posture

**Precision — closer.** The PM wants what is about to change in view *before* it lands. Work done by
means they would not have chosen is not finished.

- **Surface before starting:** approach, sequencing, tradeoffs, structure, the dispatch plan. Craft
  opinions — an abstraction's shape, naming — get solicited.
- **Ask on an *approach*, not merely a *direction*.** Routine mechanics inside an agreed approach
  stay EM-decided; asking about everything fails this posture.
- **Doubling-back tolerance: LOW.** Right on the first pass; pause at an unweighed fork.
- Every default gate fires, at the same or higher visibility.
