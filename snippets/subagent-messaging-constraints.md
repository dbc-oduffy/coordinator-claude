## Subagent Messaging Constraints

Address the dispatcher as `"main"`, not by name (`A-SESSION-IS-NAMED-BY-ITS-ROUTABLE-FROM-NAME`: `from-name`). Say "running" with a PID (`A-RUNNING-CLAIM-CARRIES-A-LIVE-PID`).
